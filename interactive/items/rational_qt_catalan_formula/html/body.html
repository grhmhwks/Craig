This item packages the finite checker for the conjectural rational q,t-Catalan formula from Graham Hawkes's 2024 paper.

The source script is Conjectures-and-Computations/qt-catalan/qt-conjecture.py. The curated script code/check_rational_qt_catalan_formula.py preserves the source step-coordinate generation and monomial-string comparison, while giving compact command-line output.

For a coprime pair (r,n), the checker generates the relevant rational Dyck paths, forms the distinguished subfamily determined by the closest point to the diagonal, and compares the positive monomial-string multiset with all generated path monomials plus the negative correction multiset.

The source example (r,n)=(7,12) generated 2,652 paths and returned PASS. The curated checker rejects non-coprime inputs, since the source conjecture is not expected to hold for non-coprime pairs in general.
